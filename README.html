AI Senior Series


AI, Clearly Explained - A Pilot Series for Retirees

AI content today comes in two flavors: beginner tutorials and corporate briefings. Retirees who have heard about AI but want a real, organized introduction, one that respects their experience and doesn't talk down to them, are left out. This four-session series is designed for them.

Live site: https://pezzbab-labs.github.io/ai-senior-series/


Why this exists

Most AI literacy content for older adults targets a general senior population and is delivered in a simplified, cautious, "product-recommendation-heavy" format.

This series is for a narrower group: professionally accomplished retirees who want real substance, hands-on practice using tools like ChatGPT and Claude, and space for the harder questions AI raises.

Building AI agents or apps is beyond the scope of these initial sessions.


What's here

  • Session 1 — Foundations, Detecting AI, and Real Risk
    What AI actually does, how to think about AI-generated content you often can't reliably detect, and how to build a verification habit that actually protects against AI-enabled scams.

  • Session 2 — Use Cases and Tools
    An introduction to AI tools for financial clarity, learning/legacy projects, and understanding medical visits, each covered with its strengths and limitations.

  • Session 3 — The Harder Questions
    A facilitated discussion on AI and creative work, age and learning, companionship, social implications, and personal ethics.

  • Session 4 — Current Topics
    Applying what your learned in Session 1 to real, recent cases: AI-generated content shaping news and public opinion, security incidents where people were scammed or manipulated without realizing AI was involved, and instances of AI acting or expanding capability in ways their own developers didn't anticipate.


Design principles behind the materials

Digital and print materials follow best practices for senior accessibility, including legible typeface, high-contrast warm accent color, avoidance of colors that aging vision struggles to discriminate, single-column layout, resizable text, standalone documents to teach on their own rather than summarize a talk.

This series is in development. Materials here are working drafts. Piloting is planned with a live audience, collecting pre/post confidence data across sessions to refine the content.


Background
