AI, Clearly Explained

A four-session series built specifically for professionally accomplished retirees who want real substance, hands-on practice using tools like ChatGPT and Claude, and space for the harder questions AI raises (rather than an oversimplified tech tutorial or a corporate briefing that talks down to them).

Pilot in progress — this series is being built and tested before it becomes available to a broader audience. Content and links are subject to change based on participant feedback.


The AI, Clearly Explained Series

Session 1 · Foundations, Detecting AI, and Real Risk

What AI actually does, how to identify AI-generated content you can no longer reliably spot, and how you can set up a habit to protect yourself from AI-enabled scams.

Sample handout for Session 1 →

Session 2 · AI Tools and Use Cases for Your Life

An introduction to currently available AI tools for financial clarity, learning and legacy projects, and understanding health related issues — with a discussion of each tool's strengths and limitations.

Handout in development.

Session 3 · The Harder AI Questions

A facilitated discussion on current issues surrounding AI, such as creative work, age and learning, companionship, and personal ethics. There are no right answers, only thoughtful exploration.

Handout in progress.

Session 4 · Applying What You've Learned to Recent Cases

Session 1's detection and verification skills applied to real cases: AI-generated content shaping news and public opinion, security incidents where people were scammed or manipulated without realizing AI was involved, and instances of AI acting or expanding capability in ways their own developers didn't anticipate.

Handout in development.
