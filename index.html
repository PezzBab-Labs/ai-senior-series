Beta · Piloting with Ashby Ponds Residents

AI, Clearly Explained

A four-session series built specifically for professionally accomplished retirees who want real substance, hands-on practice using tools like ChatGPT and Claude, and space for the harder questions AI raises rather than general oversimplified tech tutorials or a corporate briefings that talks down to them.

Pilot in progress — this course is being built and tested with this group before it becomes available to a broader audience. Content and links below are subject to change based on participant feedback

The Series

Session 1 · Foundations, Detecting AI, and Real Risk

What AI actually does, how to think about AI-generated content you can no longer reliably spot, and how you to set up the one habit that can protect you from AI-enabled scams.

Handout in Development. Sample for Session 1 available here→

Session 2 · Use Cases and Tools for Your Life

An introduction to currently available tools for financial clarity, learning and legacy projects, and understanding medical visits — each with a discussion of its strengths and limitations.

Handout in development.

Session 3 · The Harder Questions

A facilitated discussion on current issues surrounding AI, such as creative work, age and learning, companionship, and personal ethics. There are no right answers, only thoughtful exploration.

Handout in progress.

Session 4 · Applying What You've Learned: Recent Cases

Session 1's detection and verification skills applied to real, recent cases: AI-generated content shaping news and public opinion, security incidents where people were scammed or manipulated without realizing AI was involved, and instances of AI acting or expanding capability in ways their own developers didn't anticipate.

Handout in development.
